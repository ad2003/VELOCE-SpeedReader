Key Features

🌊 Natural Flow — what makes Veloce differentEvery other speed reader plays words like a metronome. Veloce actually breathes: automatic pauses at punctuation, longer time for complex words, paragraph and chapter breaks — reading rhythm the way your brain expects it.
⚖️ Word-Length DisplayDisplay time adapts to each word's actual visual width — longer words get more time automatically.
🧩 Smart ChunkingGroups words into meaningful units (e.g. "the old man") so your brain processes phrases, not just isolated words.
🖤 OLED & 5 ThemesTrue black OLED mode for AMOLED screens. Plus Dark, Light, Sepia, High Contrast and Soft themes.
🔤 Bionic ReadingBold word prefixes guide your eye to the recognition point of each word for faster scanning.
📚 All Major FormatsReads EPUB (with chapters), PDF, TXT, Markdown, HTML & FB2. Remembers your exact position per book — pick up exactly where you left off.
🌐 100% Browser-based · FreeNo install, no account, no tracking. Runs entirely in your browser. Deep timing optimization for English & German — fully adaptive for any language. Presented by quitebeyond · GitHub ↗
